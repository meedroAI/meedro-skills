## 🚀 Install

Pick the setup that matches how you use Claude.

### In the Claude app (web or desktop)

1. Open **Settings**, then the **Plugins** tab.
2. Click the **Add** button (top right), then choose **Add marketplace** from the menu.
3. Paste this into the URL field:
   ```
   meedroAI/meedro-skills
   ```
4. Turn on **Sync automatically** so new skills arrive as we publish them.
5. Click **Sync**. Meedro now appears in your plugins.

### In Claude Code (terminal)

```bash
/plugin marketplace add meedroAI/meedro-skills
```

```bash
/plugin install meedro@meedro-skills
```

---

Then type `/meedro` to see every skill, or just say **"help me find what's going viral in my niche."**

The first time you use a skill, Claude asks you to authorize your Meedro account (one click). Don't have one yet? [Start your 3-day trial](https://meedro.com) - it takes a minute.


It checks your setup, shows you the map, and walks you through your first find-to-script run step by step.

## ✨ What you can do

### 🔍 Find winners
- **`/meedro:search-viral-videos`** - search Meedro's viral library by topic and surface the outliers.
- **`/meedro:videos-watchlist`** - the top videos from the creators you track.
- **`/meedro:analyze-profile`** - a tracked creator's videos, ranked by what actually popped.

### 🧠 Understand what worked
- **`/meedro:analyze-video`** - a full breakdown of any video: the hook, the structure, the transcript, and reusable templates. See exactly why it earned the views.

### ✍️ Create in your voice
- **`/meedro:what-to-make-next`** - ranked ideas for your next video, pulled from your watchlist and the viral library.
- **`/meedro:script-generator`** - a full script, from a brief or based on a video you just analyzed.
- **`/meedro:hook-generator`** - scroll-stopping hooks on demand.
- **`/meedro:recreate-with-a-twist`** - remix a winning video into your own, same format and pacing.
- **`/meedro:templatize`** - turn any script or hook into a reusable template.

### 🗂️ Organize
Just ask - track creators in a watchlist, save scripts and videos into projects, or add notes. No command needed.
